Things Terra can't (and shouldn't) do on its own — a gift for a returning guest, a handwritten note, someone waiting for a late arrival.

The 3-days-before email links each guest to a personal page where their stay is already set up the way most guests like it. Whatever they change shows up here as a prep sheet, and anything that needs a person (extra bed, drinks to stock, experiences to arrange) becomes a team task.

The resort calendar. Guests staying during an event see it in their messages (with priority booking). Events open to everyone also go to past guests who opted in — never more than the limits in Settings.
