Guest messages

Terra prepares a warm, personal message for every guest before they arrive and after they leave — in their own language, on their preferred channel. A few days before arrival it also invites them to set up their stay, and their choices appear under Guest preferences as a prep sheet for the team. Review the wording here, edit anything you like, and Terra sends each one at the right moment.
